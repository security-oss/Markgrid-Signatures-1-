Two99 Signature Generator

How to use your signature:

  1. Pick a logo style (2×2 grid above)
  2. Click "Generate Signature" to create your signature
  3. In the new window that opens, press Ctrl+A (Windows) or Cmd+A (Mac) to select all content
  4. Press Ctrl+C (Windows) or Cmd+C (Mac) to copy
  5. Go to Gmail Settings → Signature
  6. Paste the copied content using Ctrl+V (Windows) or Cmd+V (Mac)
  7. Click "Save Changes" in Gmail
